HOW WE DELIVER QUALITY

The Arbern Engineering Methodology

From the initial AutoCAD coordinate in Kahathuduwa to the final millimeter of stone polishing, our 5-phase systematic construction workflow guarantees perfection.

PHASE 01

Soil Survey & CAD Drafting

Core soil drilling, topographic contour survey, zoning approvals, and comprehensive 2D/3D architectural drafting.

DURATION: 3-4 WEEKS
PHASE 02

Structural & BOQ Auditing

Finite element structural modeling, seismic calculations, rebar scheduling, and itemized SLS 573 BOQ preparation.

DURATION: 2-3 WEEKS
PHASE 03

Substructure & Foundation

Earth retention, bored pile driving or pad footings, grade beam casting, and multi-layer moisture/radon waterproofing.

DURATION: 6-8 WEEKS
PHASE 04

Superstructure & Smart MEP

Post-tensioned slab pours, column grids, thermal break aluminum glazing, and smart 3-phase electrical/plumbing routing.

DURATION: 12-16 WEEKS
PHASE 05

Bespoke Finish & Handover

Architectural stone installation, acoustic lighting calibration, landscape commissioning, and Certificate of Conformity (COC).

DURATION: 6-8 WEEKS
